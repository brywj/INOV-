About Us

Trusted Partner in Engineering Excellence

INOV Consulting Engineer Sdn Bhd is a Civil and Structural Engineering Consultancy established in 2024, registered with the Board of Engineers Malaysia (BEM).

Led by Ir. Ng Wen Yeh, our Managing Director with over 20 years of experience, we specialise in Civil, Structural, Geotechnical Engineering and Project Management consultancy services.

We prioritise good engineering practices — ensuring every design proposal is optimised for structural integrity, cost efficiency, and timely project completion.
